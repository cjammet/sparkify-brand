About
I’m Christophe Jammet. Most people call me CJ.
I was born and raised in New York, and I’ve spent more than fifteen years helping brands like IBM, AMD and Riot Games say something worth hearing. Today I lead emerging media and technology at Gather, and I take on a small number of advisory clients through Sparkify.
My work sits where reputation, content and AI meet. I care about one question more than any other: does this make life better for the person on the other end?
